# System Architecture Documentation

## Overview
Aemade Selam Sunday School Management System (አዕማደ ሰላም ሰንበት ትምህርት ቤት) is designed to serve:
- Administrative staff & clergy (super_admin, admin)
- Teachers & instructors (teacher)
- Content managers (content_manager)
- Sunday school students & church community (member)
- Public visitors (visitor)

## Modules Planned
- Authentication & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Members & Profile Management
- Academic & Classes Management (Attendance, Grades)
- Sunday School Departments & Activities
- Content & Media Management (Articles, Announcements, Media)
- Reporting & Document Generation (Puppeteer PDF, Word, Excel)
